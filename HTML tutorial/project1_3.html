DADABHAI NAOROJI LIBRARY

Dadabhai Naoroji Library, Ankola

    History

  1. * The library was established along with Gokhale Centenary College in **1966**.
  2. * Initially, it functioned in a classroom of the college.
  3. * In **1970**, the foundation stone for an independent library building was laid.
  4. * The new library building was inaugurated on **3 October 1971**.
  5. * The separate library building reflects the vision of the college's founder, **Dr. Dinakar Desai**, who believed that a library should be the academic heart of the institution

The library plays a vital role in supporting teaching, learning, and research at Gokhale Centenary College.
     It provides students with access to quality academic resources, encourages reading habits, and supports
      self-learning through both printed and digital materials. The college's reports describe it as a major
       academic resource with a dedicated building and modern library services.
