**Introduction – Gokhale Centenary Pre-University College, Ankola**

Gokhale Centenary Pre-University College, Ankola, one of the leading educational institutions in Uttara Kannada district, Karnataka. Managed by the **Kanara Welfare Trust**, the college became an independent Pre-University institution in **June 2001** following the bifurcation of PU and degree colleges by the Government of Karnataka. Located on National Highway 66 at Vandige, Ankola, the college offers **Science and Commerce** streams at the Pre-University level and is known for its quality education, disciplined environment, and experienced faculty. The institution provides well-equipped laboratories, a computer laboratory, library facilities, and opportunities for students to participate in sports, cultural activities, and other co-curricular programs. The college is committed to developing students' academic excellence, leadership qualities, moral values, and social responsibility. Over the years, Gokhale Centenary Pre-University College has earned a reputation for producing excellent academic results and preparing students for higher education and successful careers.
