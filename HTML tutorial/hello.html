BEAUTIFUL GREEN CAMPUS IN G.C.PU.COLLEGE

Campus life at **Gokhale Centenary College, Ankola** is known for its friendly atmosphere, academic focus, and opportunities for extracurricular development. Established in **1966**, the college provides a peaceful environment that supports both learning and personal growth.

  • * Cultural programmes, annual day celebrations, and youth festivals.
  • * Sports such as volleyball, kabaddi, handball, netball, and athletics.
  • * NCC, NSS, Youth Red Cross, and other student clubs that promote leadership and community service.
  • * Seminars, workshops, personality development programmes, and career guidance activities organized by the Career Guidance.
